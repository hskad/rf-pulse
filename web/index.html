PHYSICAL OBSERVATIONS

Physical barriers dictate connection quality.

Three core realities discovered across 360 physical campus measurements.

  1. 001.

    Hostel Living Quarters

    Enclosed Rooms Absorb Over Half of Signal Power

    Interior walls and closed partitions absorb up to +12.8 dBm of signal, dropping physical link speed from 144 Mbps down to 58 Mbps.

  2. 002.

    Open Corridors

    Signals Travel Well in Hallways

    Radio waves propagate cleanly down open corridors, but lose substantial power when passing through concrete room walls.

  3. 003.

    Common Areas

    Canteen & Meeting Room Deadzones

    Located 75–95 meters away from the nearest router, severe lag spikes cause UPI payment timeouts and dropped calls.
